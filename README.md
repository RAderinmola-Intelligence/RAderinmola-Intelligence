# Roy Aderinmola

*Also published as Robert Aderinmola.*

Analytics and strategy professional working on **early-warning analytics for financial stability**: how behavioral and market data can give financial institutions earlier, better-validated signals of emerging risk. Background in customer intelligence, market intelligence and analytics across banking, telecom, trading and investment, and fintech.

## Focus areas
- Predictive analytics for financial-risk monitoring
- Behavioral and consumer-sentiment data as early-warning signals
- Cross-border financial stress and nonbank leverage
- Data analytics and BI: Python, SQL, Power BI

## Selected work
**[ifrmef-backtest](https://github.com/RAderinmola-Intelligence/ifrmef-backtest)**: reproducible back-test of whether consumer sentiment improves early warning of financial stress, using public data. First test found no evidence of improvement; a follow-up is pre-registered in the repository.
- **Reading notes on Fed, FSOC and IMF financial-stability reports**: planned
